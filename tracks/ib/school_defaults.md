# IB MYP — starting defaults

Setup copies this into `00_school/` when the program (A8) is IB MYP, then asks
the person to **confirm or change** each line. Everything marked *(school
choice)* differs between schools. These are starting points, not rules.

## What every MYP school shares

- **Criteria:** four per subject, A–D. Each is marked 0–8.
- **Final grade:** the four criterion levels are added (0–32) and converted to
  a grade from 1 to 7 using the IB's published boundaries.
- **Objectives by year:** the subject guides publish objectives for Year 1,
  Year 3 and Year 5. MYP 1 uses Year 1 and MYP 3 uses Year 3. MYP 5 uses Year 5.
  MYP 2 and MYP 4 use one of the years either side: **a school setting, the
  same for every subject** *(school choice; ask)*.
- **Every unit plan has:** a key concept, related concepts, a global context,
  a statement of inquiry, inquiry questions (factual, conceptual, debatable),
  ATL skills, the objectives (criteria and strands) it assesses, and a
  summative task.
- **Criteria names** for all eight subject groups are in `criteria/`. They come
  from the IB's public subject briefs. **Strand wording (i, ii, iii…) comes
  from the school's own licensed subject guide**, and setup asks for it.

## School choices to confirm (from one working example)
