# Sources (question C5)

Textbooks, syllabi and resources your school is **licensed to use**. Put the
files here yourself. The AI never downloads a textbook for you.

One folder per subject is easiest: `02_sources/<subject>/`.

Content, figures and quotations in generated work come only from files here,
or from general knowledge, and every one names its source.

**This folder is never published.** `.gitignore` keeps its contents out of git,
because textbooks are copyrighted.
