# Make a worksheet

**Typed:** `make a worksheet` · **Needs:** an approved lesson
· **Saves to:** `06_outputs/<teacher>/<unit>/L<nn>_worksheet.md` (and `.docx` at Level 2)

The worksheet is what students do. It follows the lesson's plan section by
section. It's not a summary of the slides.

## Read first

The approved lesson, its unit's `1_outline.md` (for criteria and strands),
`00_school/house_style.md` (B8) and `03_resources/classes.md` (C10).

## Format

```markdown
# <Lesson title>
Name: ____________  Class: ______  Date: ______

## <Section title> · <minutes> min
Inquiry question: <the question this section answers>

<Any table or data students need comes ABOVE the questions that use it,
and the questions say "use the table above".>

1. <Question> (<criterion and strand, if assessed>) · <minutes>
   a) …
   b) …
```

## Rules

- **Thinking, not copying.** No question may be answered by copying a
  sentence from the slides. Mix the question types:
  - **factual:** what is it?
  - **conceptual:** why or how does it work?
  - **debatable:** what should we do, and why?

  The last question in each section answers that section's inquiry question.
- **Sized to the time, not to a count.** Plan the minutes per question:
  recall 1–2 · describe or calculate 3 · explain or analyze 5 · evaluate or
  design 8 · plot a graph 10 · each blank table cell about 0.4. A section's
  questions fill its minutes from the lesson plan, within a minute or two.
- **I do and We do are on the worksheet**, not in a notebook. I do is a table
  students fill in while the teacher thinks aloud. We do is answered together.
  Neither uses the items the main task tests.
- **Every question is required.** No optional challenge. Each section of ten
  minutes or more ends on its hardest question.
- **Frames:** full sentence frames for younger students (or when A10 says
  students are learning in an additional language), short starters for older
  ones. A frame never does the thinking for them.
- **Word bank** at the top of every lesson if A10 says so (English only,
  unless house style says otherwise).
- **Assessed questions** name their criterion and strand after the question,
  e.g. "(C.iii)". In a formative task, every strand of the criterion is
  assessed by at least one question.
- **Layout:** one part (a), (b), (c) per line. No answer box where a table
  students fill in is the answer.
- **No homework** for grades whose policy (B7) says none.

Then run `05_checks/checks.md`, report, and ask for approval.
