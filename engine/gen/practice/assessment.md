# Forces · Summative assessment
Status: DRAFT · Step 3 of 3

## The task
The Lakeside market association wants carts that are easier to push. You will test how the mass of a toy cart changes how far it rolls, and advise them.

## Questions or parts
| # | What students do | Criterion and strand | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Describe the forces on a cart being pushed at a steady speed | K.1 | 5 |
| 2 | Explain why a heavier cart is harder to start moving | K.2 | 5 |
| 3 | Write your question and prediction for the ramp test | I.1 | 5 |
| 4 | Run the test and record your own results | I.2 | 15 |
| 5 | Present your results in a table and a graph | C.1 | 10 |
| 6 | Conclude and say how sure you are, using force vocabulary | I.3, C.2 | 10 |

## Data or materials each student gets
Each student records their own three trials in their own table.
