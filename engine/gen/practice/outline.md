# Science · Grade 7 · Term 1 · Forces
Status: DRAFT · Step 1 of 3

> Practice run: a worked example for the sample school, not written by an AI.

## Big ideas
Forces are pushes and pulls we can measure. When the forces on an object are balanced, its motion stays the same; when they are unbalanced, its speed or direction changes.

## Inquiry questions
| Type | Question |
|---|---|
| Factual | What units do we measure force in? |
| Conceptual | Why does a ferry keep moving at the same speed when its engine pushes as hard as the water drags? |
| Debatable | Should market carts in Lakeside be made lighter or given bigger wheels? |
