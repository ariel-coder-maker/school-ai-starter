# Lesson 3 · Balanced and unbalanced
Status: DRAFT
Unit: Forces · Grade 7 · Taught minutes: 50 · Kind: concept

## Objective
Students can explain whether the forces on an object are balanced, using its motion as evidence.

## Inquiry question
Why does a ferry keep a steady speed?

## Bridge
From last lesson: students measured pulls with spring balances in newtons.
Toward the summative: this builds K.2, explaining the forces on the market cart.

## Plan
| # | Section | Minutes | Kind of work | What the teacher does | What students do |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ferry photo | 4 | hook | Shows a ferry at steady speed; asks what pushes it | Turn and talk |
| 2 | Last lesson | 4 | bridge | Asks for one reading from last lesson | Share a reading |
| 3 | Balanced forces | 8 | explain | Draws force arrows on a box: balanced and unbalanced | Copy the two arrow pictures |
| 4 | A bicycle taxi | 6 | i-do | Thinks aloud through the forces on a bicycle taxi | Fill in the I do table |
| 5 | A rolling ball | 6 | we-do | Asks the class to decide the forces on a slowing ball | Answer together on the worksheet |
| 6 | Toy cars | 14 | investigate | Circulates; asks why the car slows | Push cars on two surfaces, record and explain |
| 7 | New context | 6 | apply-new-context | Shows a market cart at rest | Explain the forces on the cart |
| 8 | Exit question | 2 | exit | Collects answers | Answer the exit question |

Total: 50

## New words
| Word | Plain explanation | Example | Non-example | Practised in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| balanced | equal forces in opposite directions | a ferry at steady speed | a car speeding up | question 1 |
| unbalanced | one force is bigger | a ball slowing down | a book resting on a desk | question 3 |

## Check for understanding
Thumbs up or down on four motion pictures before section 6.

## Equipment and set-up
6 toy cars, 6 meter rulers, masking tape for a 1 m track on two surfaces. No safety risk beyond cars on the floor.

## Differentiation
Word bank at the top; sentence frame "The forces are ___ because the motion ___".

## Every required planner field
Objective, inquiry question, plan with minutes, check for understanding: all above.
