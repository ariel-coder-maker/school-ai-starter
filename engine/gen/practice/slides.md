# Balanced and unbalanced
Status: DRAFT

## Slide 1 · agenda · Start
Today
- Ferry puzzle
- Force arrows
- Toy cars on two surfaces

Figure: none

Teacher note: Read the three parts aloud and point to the inquiry question on the board: why does a ferry keep a steady speed? Tell students they will answer it by the end.

---
## Slide 2 · hook · Ferry photo
What pushes a ferry along, and what holds it back?

Figure: a river ferry at steady speed (school photo)

Teacher note: Give thirty seconds of silent thinking, then turn and talk. Collect two ideas, engine and water. Do not correct yet; come back to this at the end of the lesson.

---
## Slide 3 · explain · Balanced forces
Balanced: equal and opposite
Unbalanced: one is bigger

Figure: two box diagrams with force arrows

Teacher note: Draw the box with two equal arrows and ask whether it speeds up. Then make one arrow longer and ask again. Stress that balanced means the motion stays the same, which can mean moving steadily.

---
## Slide 4 · i-do · A bicycle taxi
Forces on a bicycle taxi at steady speed

Figure: bicycle taxi with two arrows

Teacher note: Think aloud: the pedals push forward, friction and air push back, and the speed is not changing, so the forces must be equal. Students fill in the I do table as you talk.

---
## Slide 5 · we-do · A rolling ball
The ball slows down. Which force is bigger?

Figure: ball rolling on grass

Teacher note: Ask pairs to decide, then cold-call three pairs. Agree together that friction is bigger, so the forces are unbalanced and the ball slows. Students write this on the worksheet.

---
## Slide 6 · check · Quick check
Thumbs up: balanced. Thumbs down: unbalanced.
- A parked car
- A car speeding up

Figure: none

Teacher note: Read each picture and wait for every thumb. Re-teach with the box arrows if more than a few are wrong.

---
## Slide 7 · instructions · Toy cars
Push the car the same way on the desk and on carpet. Three trials each. Record in the table.

Figure: none

Teacher note: Model one gentle push. Groups of four, one recorder. Remind them that a fair test uses the same push each time.

---
## Slide 8 · summary · What we found
Steady motion: balanced forces
Changing motion: unbalanced forces

Figure: none

Teacher note: Return to the ferry: the engine push equals the water drag, so the forces are balanced and the speed stays the same.

---
## Slide 9 · exit · Exit question
A cart at rest starts to move. What changed in the forces?

Figure: none

Teacher note: Collect answers on the way out; sort them into explained and not explained to plan lesson 4.
