# Assessment and planners

## B5 Criteria and grading scale

- Scale: each strand marked 1–4 (beginning, developing, secure, extending)
- How a final grade is reached: the most consistent level per criterion across the term
- What a rubric must look like: one column per strand, four levels, written to the student

## B6 Planner platform and required fields

- Platform: Google Classroom
- Fields every **unit** plan must fill: big ideas, inquiry questions, what is assessed, standards
- Fields every **lesson** plan must fill: objective, inquiry question, plan with minutes, check for understanding
