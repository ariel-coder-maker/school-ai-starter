## B2 Learning weeks per term

## B3 Periods

- Period length on the timetable: 55 minutes
- Minutes actually taught (what every lesson is timed to): 50 minutes
- Lessons per week, per subject: 2

## B4 Unit shape

- Units per term (or per year): 2 per term
- Lessons per unit: 10
- Summatives must be finished by: the end of week 4 of the unit
- Where summatives sit in the unit, and how many lessons they take: lesson 8, one lesson

## The sum

10 learning weeks × 2 lessons a week = 20 lessons = 2 units × 10 lessons. It adds up.
